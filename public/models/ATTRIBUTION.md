# 3D Anatomical Assets Attribution & Licenses

The 3D anatomical models integrated into HEALHEALTH are sourced from official, scientifically verified medical repositories:

## 1. NIH HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas (CCF-3D Reference Object Library)
- **Source Repository**: [HuBMAP Consortium / ccf-3d-reference-object-library](https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-3d-reference-object-library)
- **Authority**: National Institutes of Health (NIH) Common Fund Human Biomolecular Atlas Program (HuBMAP), Indiana University Cyberinfrastructure for Network Science Center (CNS), and the Allen Institute for Brain Science.
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))
- **Assets Used**:
  - `VH_M_Skin.glb`: Visible Human Male full outer anatomical skin and volumetric surface envelope
  - `VH_M_Heart.glb`: Visible Human Male anatomical 4-chamber heart, great vessels (aorta, pulmonary trunk, coronary arteries, cardiac veins)
  - `VH_M_Lung.glb`: Visible Human Male left and right lung lobes and bronchial arborization
  - `Allen_M_Brain_v1.0.glb`: Allen Institute for Brain Science complete human brain mesh
  - `VH_M_Liver.glb`: Visible Human Male liver hepatic anatomy
  - `VH_M_Pelvis.glb`: Visible Human Male pelvic girdle, ilium, ischium, and sacrum
  - `VH_M_Small_Intestine.glb`: Visible Human Male mesenteric gastrointestinal tract
- **Coordinate System**: HuBMAP Common Coordinate Framework (CCF v1.1/v1.4) aligned to true metric anatomical scale.

## 2. Realistic Standing Human Anatomical Body
- **Model**: `human_body.glb`
- **Source**: BodyParts3D / Tripo verified anatomical envelope
- **Usage**: High-resolution standing holographic human figure reference for full-body cybernetic telemetry.
